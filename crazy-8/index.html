Card guide: what every card does
2
Next player picks up 2. They can chain another 2 or a joker, or block with an Ace.
Joker
Next player picks up 5. Chains with 2s and jokers; an Ace blocks it. Goes on any card.
Ace
Blocks a pick-up of any size. After an Ace, the next player can play any card.
J
Jump: the next player is skipped.
7
Reverses the direction of play.
