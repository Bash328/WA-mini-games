Summary

WA mini-games has no passwords and no email sign-in. You can optionally pick a username (see below). Playing by link needs no server at all: the site is static files and nothing about your games is stored on our end. The optional live online mode (see below) does use a small relay server so two people can play in real time.

How the games work

Each game's state (the board, whose turn it is, and so on) is encoded into the page's URL. When you send a move to a friend, you're sending them a link — nothing is transmitted to us or stored on a server. Who you send that link to, and how (WhatsApp, or any other way), is entirely up to you and happens outside this site.

Live online play (optional)

If you choose a game's "online" mode, creating a game opens a short-lived room on a relay run on Cloudflare. The relay receives the moves and board state you send, passes them to your opponent, and keeps the room only while you play: up to 24 hours if left idle, or 1 hour after the game finishes, after which it is deleted. There are no names or accounts. A random token kept in your browser's local storage identifies your seat so you can reconnect. Anyone who has a room's link or code can take the open seat, so share it only with the person you want to play. Cloudflare processes connection details such as IP addresses to operate the service; we don't store them.

Username, notifications and pokes (optional)

On the home page you can pick a username so a friend can "poke" you into a live game. There is no password and no email: the name is kept by a relay run on Cloudflare, together with a secret code that stays in your browser and proves the name is yours. If you clear your browser's site data you lose the name, and we cannot recover it.

What is stored: the username; a hash of the secret code (not the code itself); the time you last used the site (a name unused for 90 days is deleted); if you turn notifications on, your browser's push subscription (an address at Google's, Mozilla's, Apple's or Microsoft's push service and the keys to encrypt messages for it); your mute setting and the usernames you block; and a few timestamps used to limit how many pokes can be sent and received. Usernames are visible to other players only as the name on a poke.

What a poke does: when someone pokes you, we send an encrypted message through your browser's push service saying who poked you and linking to their live room. Only your browser can read it. Someone you have blocked, or who has been poking too often, will not get through; they are not told which.

Deleting it: use "Release this name" on the home page and the record, including your push subscription, is deleted straight away. Turning notifications off removes just the subscription. You can also mute pokes or block a name there.

What's stored on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember things like your username and its secret code, your theme preference, difficulty settings, and which games you've already seen the moves for — so a stale link can be recognized. None of this is sent anywhere; it stays on your device and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to see aggregate traffic — things like which games are played and how many people visit — and to help Google measure and personalize ads (see Advertising above). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and which pages you visit; it does not see your name, email, or any of the moves you make in a game. You can opt out of Google Analytics for all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or block it with a privacy-focused browser extension.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
