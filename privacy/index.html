Summary

WA mini-games has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. There's nothing to log in to and nothing stored about you on our end, because we don't run a backend at all — the site is static files.

How the games work

Each game's state (the board, whose turn it is, and so on) is encoded into the page's URL. When you send a move to a friend, you're sending them a link — nothing is transmitted to us or stored on a server. Who you send that link to, and how (WhatsApp, or any other way), is entirely up to you and happens outside this site.

What's stored on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember things like your theme preference, difficulty settings, and which games you've already seen the moves for — so a stale link can be recognized. None of this is sent anywhere; it stays on your device and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

Advertising

We intend to show ads on this site through Google AdSense. When active, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technology to serve ads and measure their performance, which can include using data about your visits to this and other sites to personalize the ads you see. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to see aggregate traffic — things like which games are played and how many people visit — and to help Google measure and personalize ads (see Advertising above). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and which pages you visit; it does not see your name, email, or any of the moves you make in a game. You can opt out of Google Analytics for all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or block it with a privacy-focused browser extension.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example as advertising is turned on. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
